\item Kites, wing sails and rotors compared on the same ships, routes and weather.
\item \stale{classes}{} \tbd{Kites lead on container ships; kite and rotors are level on bulk carriers and car carriers.}
\item \stale{classes}{} At their goals the best rig saves \tbd{12 to 21}\,\% of the cost on e-ammonia, wings half.
\item \stale{classes}{} On fuel oil the best rig, a kite or rotors, saves \tbd{5 to 8}\,\% of the cost, up to \tbd{15}\,\%.
\item \stale{situations_data}{} Another e-fuel at the same price, such as e-methanol, changes the cost savings little.
\item The kite needs its canopy-life and no-crash goals. VTOL kites cost too much.
