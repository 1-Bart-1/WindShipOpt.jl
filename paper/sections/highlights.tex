\item Kites, wing sails and rotors compared on the same ships, routes and weather.
\item Kites on container ships, rotors on car carriers, either on bulk carriers and tankers.
\item At their goals the best rig saves \tbd{11 to 13}\,\% of the cost on e-ammonia, wings half.
\item On fuel oil the best rig, mostly a kite, saves \tbd{2 to 6}\,\% of the cost, up to \tbd{9}\,\%.
\item Another e-fuel at the same price, such as e-methanol, changes the cost savings little.
\item The kite needs its canopy-life and no-crash goals. VTOL kites cost too much.
