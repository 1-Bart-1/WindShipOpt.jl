\item Kites, wing sails and rotors compared on the same ships, routes and weather.
\item Rotors lead on bulk carriers, tankers and car carriers; kites on container ships on fuel oil.
\item At their goals the best rig saves \tbd{7 to 16}\,\% of the cost on e-ammonia, wings half.
\item On fuel oil the best rig, a kite or rotors, saves \tbd{1 to 5}\,\% of the cost, up to \tbd{9}\,\%.
\item Another e-fuel at the same price, such as e-methanol, changes the cost savings little.
\item The kite needs its canopy-life and no-crash goals. VTOL kites cost too much.
