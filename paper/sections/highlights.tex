\item Kites, wing sails and rotors compared on the same ships, routes and weather.
\item If each rig reaches its goals, kites and rotors save about \tbd{a tenth} on e-methanol.
\item On fuel oil the best rig saves \tbd{2 to 6}\,\% of the cost, up to \tbd{9}\,\%; wing sails save about half of kites and rotors.
\item Kites lead on container ships, rotors on the car carrier; it depends on the route.
\item The kite needs its canopy-life and no-crash goals to keep up with the rotors.
